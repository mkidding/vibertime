## 🛑 The Problem: "The 4AM Loop"

We all know the meme: _"Sleeping is for the weak."_ It's funny on Reddit, but in reality, it's a burnout trap.

Vibe coding is addictive. It hits the same dopamine buttons as gambling—"Just one more prompt and it will work." You feel like a wizard, chaining LLM outputs and watching code appear from thin air.

Next thing you know, it's dawn, your eyes are burning, and you're debugging a race condition that didn't exist six hours ago.

## ⚡ The Solution

**ViberTime** is a Retro-Futuristic monitor designed to keep you in the flow state _without_ letting you drown in it.

### 🤖 Feature: Accomplishment Analysis
**See how much you and your AI accomplished together.**

*   **Biological Input**: Tracks your physical keyboard strokes.
*   **Synthetic Input**: Tracks code generated by AI or pasted.
*   **The Balance**: Tracks your time spent coding and reviewing.

### 🔋 Feature: Bedtime Reminder
**Your energy isn't infinite.**

*   **Target Bedtime**: Set a "Hard Stop" for your session.
*   **The Gentle Nudge**: The UI gently reminds you when it's time to disconnect.

### 🎰 Feature: Viber Slot Machine (Vibe Predict)
**Coding with AI feels like a gamble.**

Sometimes it's magic, sometimes it's hallucinations.

*   **Spin the Wheel**: Run the Slot Machine once a day to "predict" your vibe.
*   **Just for Fun**: A retro mini-game to break the tension of debugging.

## 🚀 Usage

**Quick Access**:
Click the clock indicator `[ 🕒 00:00 ]` in the **Status Bar** (bottom right) to open the Dashboard instantly.

**Command Palette**:
Or run `ViberTime: Show Dashboard` (`Ctrl+Shift+P` / `Cmd+Shift+P`).

## 🔒 Our Privacy & Data Promise

**ViberTime (Community Edition)** is designed to be **Strictly Offline**

* **100% Local:** ViberTime only tracks events but not details. Your stats, code metrics, and sessions are stored strictly on your machine in the global storage.

* **Forever Free:** This Community Edition is and will always be free for individual developers.
